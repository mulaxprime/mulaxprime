About Me

Why I Build
What I Build

My name is Amantle Mpaekae, also known as Mulax Prime. I'm a self-taught developer from Mogoditshane, Botswana, working toward becoming an AI Engineer.

I scored a D in Computer Studies for BGCSE. That same subject is what I now build software for. Mothusi exists because I know what it feels like to be stuck on a topic with no one to explain it simply — so I built the explainer I needed. Tsela exists because I went through the university admissions process myself with no reliable source for points requirements and deadlines — so I built that too, offline, for the next student going through the same thing.

I learn by shipping, not by studying theory first. Every version of Mothusi and every constraint on Tsela taught me something no course could — debugging real crashes, fixing real architecture problems, demoing to real teachers, and getting real feedback.

Mothusi — BGCSE Study Assistant

An AI voice and chat study assistant for BGCSE Computer Studies students in Botswana. Students type or speak a question and get an answer read aloud in a natural voice. Includes a full quiz mode — 7 questions, 10 marks, two attempts per question, and automatic score saving. Built with Python, tkinter, Groq AI, edge-tts and SpeechRecognition. Demoed live at Mogoditshane Senior Secondary School.

Offline AIADTC 2026

Tsela — Offline University Admissions Assistant

An offline, on-device AI assistant answering university admissions questions for six Botswana institutions. Runs a quantized Qwen2.5-1.5B model via llama.cpp on an 8GB no-GPU laptop, with a deterministic lookup that bypasses the model entirely for points and requirement questions — preventing hallucination on the numbers that matter most. Built and submitted solo for the Africa Deep Tech Challenge 2026.
